# mni152.nii.gz

The ICBM 152 Nonlinear Symmetric 2009a T1 template (1x1x1mm, 197x233x189),
included here as a small (~1.6MB) demo volume so the viewer works without
needing your own data. Obtained via the `nilearn` package
(`nilearn.datasets.load_mni152_template()`), which redistributes it from the
McConnell Brain Imaging Centre.
